# Offline-authenticated cold start

If your refresh token has genuinely expired — most commonly after the app has
been closed and offline for a while — you are no longer bounced to the
sign-in screen. You stay signed in for local purposes: your cached songs and
plans remain fully visible, you can keep reading and editing, and a banner
lets you know sync is paused. Once you're back online and re-authenticate
through that banner, sync resumes automatically and picks up anything you
changed while offline.
